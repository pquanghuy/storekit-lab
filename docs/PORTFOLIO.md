## StoreKit Lab

SwiftUI · StoreKit 2 · StoreKitTest · XCTest

An independent iOS demo of a one-time purchase with verified entitlement handling, explicit restoration, and transaction updates. Includes local StoreKit configuration and two passing simulator UI tests covering purchase, relaunch, restore, refund, and deferred approval.

The implementation rebuilds access from StoreKit rather than persisting a purchase flag, and acknowledges transactions after updating access. A case study documents the design and the limits of local testing.
